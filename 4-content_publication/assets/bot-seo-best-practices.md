## **SEO Best Practices**

1. **Keyword Integration**  
   * Get primary keywords from the content idea, integrate it into the title of the article and in the first 100 words of the article.  
   * Analyze top-performing articles to get [long-tail keywords and short-tail keywords](https://ahrefs.com/blog/long-tail-vs-short-tail-keywords/). Then integrate these keywords into the body and headers of the articles.  
2. **Structure Optimization**  
   * One H1 title → H2 section headers → H3 subheaders  
   * 700-800 words per main section (informed by top articles)  
   * Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)  
3. **Content Enrichment**  
   * 2-3 relevant [internal](https://yoast.com/internal-linking-for-seo-why-and-how/) or [external links](https://www.klipfolio.com/resources/kpi-examples/digital-marketing/external-links).  
   * Readability at Grade 7 level  
   * Include 1 contextually relevant image
